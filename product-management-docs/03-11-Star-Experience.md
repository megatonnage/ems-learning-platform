### What is an 11-Star Experience?

Based on Brian Chesky's framework (Airbnb), the 11-star experience goes beyond satisfaction to create something magical and world-changing.

---

## The Star Levels

### 1-3 Stars: Basic Expectations
- [ ] Platform loads
- [ ] Questions work
- [ ] Answers are correct

### 4-5 Stars: Good Experience
- [ ] Clean interface
- [ ] Organized content
- [ ] Mobile-friendly

### 6 Stars: Pleasant Surprise
- [ ] Adaptive difficulty
- [ ] Progress tracking
- [ ] Study streaks with fun animations

### 7 Stars: Delightful
- [ ] Clever mnemonics
- [ ] Personalized study plans
- [ ] Celebration animations when mastering topics

### 8 Stars: Exceptional
- [ ] Scenario-based cases that feel like real calls
- [ ] Peer comparison ("You're in top 10% for pediatric!")
- [ ] Voice-guided studying for hands-free review

### 9 Stars: Life-Changing
- [ ] Confidence boost - users say "I passed because of this"
- [ ] Clinical instinct development - pattern recognition
- [ ] Stress reduction - "I felt prepared for my exam"

### 10-11 Stars: World-Changing/Magical
- [ ] Saves actual lives - "I remembered this protocol in the field"
- [ ] Career transformation - From EMT to Paramedic with confidence
- [ ] Community of excellence - Users mentoring each other
