## Key Principles

1. **Start with user problems, not solutions**
2. **Ship fast, iterate based on feedback**
3. **Measure what matters**
4. **Document decisions**

---

## Decision-Making Framework

**When choosing what to build:**
- Does it align with our Product Principles (Most Fun, Most Helpful)?
- What's the user impact?
- What's the effort required?
- Can we validate quickly?
