📖 Instructions for Using Filters

The filters may seem confusing at first, but they will make sense as you use them. Higher certification levels need to know content from more junior levels. For example:

  • AEMTs must know everything from the EMT level
  • Paramedics must know everything from BOTH EMT and AEMT levels

Recommendation: Start by going through all questions at your default level. When you want to focus on specific topics, use the filters. Click "Reset All" to return to viewing all questions for your level, including those from lower certification levels.
